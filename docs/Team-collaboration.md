# Team collaboration

## Invitations

The super admin chooses the person’s email, role and brands in Team access, then creates an invitation. The hub returns a private, single-use link bound to that email and valid for 48 hours. No email is sent automatically. Copy the link, or choose Prepare invitation email to open a draft in your own email app, review it and send it. The teammate opens the link, uses the invited email, and chooses their own password. They do not need social-platform passwords. Pending invitations can be revoked; creating a replacement invalidates the previous pending link.

## Reviews

Any teammate with access to the post’s brand can add comments or flag a particular issue in the review discussion. The assigned reviewer (or an eligible super admin) can approve/complete feedback, request changes, or reject the current version. Changes and rejection require a written reason. The reason remains in the activity history and latest review note, with the content version. A rejection retains the draft so it can be revised.

Feedback is optional and does not block publishing. An explicit approval hold blocks delivery until that version is approved or a review participant deliberately cancels the hold. Self-approval is prevented. Editing content invalidates a previous approval. Reviews never publish automatically.

## Team space

Discussions belong to a brand, with question, request, observation or update categories. Optionally link a saved post or ad build. Discuss with team on a saved ad build opens a prefilled discussion. Replies are retained with the discussion. The author or super admin can mark a discussion resolved and reopen it; all authorized brand teammates can reply. Unread updates and read acknowledgements are shown in the hub, not sent as emails or push notifications.

Weekly reports use Monday-start weeks and one report per author, brand and week. Use a private draft while writing, then Share report. Even super admins cannot read another person’s unshared draft. Reports cover work/outcomes, observations/lessons, requests/blockers and next week’s priorities. Only the author can edit the report; others can reply. Every shared revision is retained, and a shared report cannot silently be turned back into a private draft. Draft content before first publication is not exposed through revision history.

The archive can be searched across report text and author names, filtered by brand, month, status or author, and exported as a text digest using those filters. Months include weeks whose Monday falls within the selected month. Export includes the matching records you are authorized to read, including your own drafts when the filter includes them. Newly assigned teammates can read historical shared material for their brands; removing brand access removes that access. There is no cross-brand public channel.

Lists paginate in groups of 40. The latest 500 replies and latest 50 shared revisions are displayed per item; older records remain stored. The reference picker lists the latest 100 posts/ad builds for the selected brand. Export requires narrower filters above 10,040 reports. No automatic recurring reports, AI-generated performance claims or live advertising actions are implied.
